Where we are, and where we're headed.

Here's exactly how we get from idea to a fully launched organization — no vague timelines, just the four real stops ahead of us.

  1. 01
    Phase 1

    Vision

    Complete. The mission was defined: close the support gap for adults diagnosed later in life.

  2. You are here
    02
    Phase 2

    Building Our Foundation

    In progress. Raising the funds and doing the groundwork to file officially — including some hands-on fundraising, like our small keychain shop.

  3. 03
    Phase 3

    501(c)(3) Filing

    Up next. Official nonprofit status, unlocking the ability to accept tax-deductible donations and apply for grants.

  4. 04
    Phase 4

    Resource Hub Launch

    The destination. A full library of guides, community connections, and practical tools built specifically for late-diagnosed adults.
